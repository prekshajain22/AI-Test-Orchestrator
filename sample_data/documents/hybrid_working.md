# Hybrid Working Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the expectations, guidelines, and eligibility for hybrid working arrangements. It is intended to support flexibility while ensuring productivity, collaboration, and a safe working environment.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff who are eligible for hybrid working arrangements at the company.

## Hybrid Working Definition
Hybrid working allows eligible team members to split their time between working remotely and working from a company office or approved workspace.

## Eligibility
Eligible employees must:
- Have a role that supports remote and in-office work
- Demonstrate reliable performance and effective communication
- Maintain access to required technology and secure internet connectivity
- Obtain approval from their manager before starting a hybrid arrangement

## Work Schedule
- Employees should coordinate with their manager and team to establish a regular hybrid schedule.
- Core collaboration hours should be defined by each team and communicated clearly.
- Employees must be available and responsive during agreed-upon work hours.

## Office Attendance
- Employees should attend the office on days designated for team meetings, training, or collaboration.
- Managers may require specific in-office days for team alignment, onboarding, or project work.
- Employees should provide advance notice if they cannot attend scheduled office days.

## Equipment and Workspace
- Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe, ergonomic home workspace.
- The company may provide or reimburse approved equipment, subject to policy and manager approval.
- Employees must follow company guidelines for the use and protection of devices, data, and confidential information.

## Communication and Collaboration
- Employees should maintain regular communication with their manager, team, and stakeholders.
- Meetings should be attended on time and prepared, regardless of location.
- Collaboration tools and company systems should be used in accordance with established practices.

## Security and Confidentiality
- Employees must adhere to company security policies, including data protection, device management, and secure network use.
- Confidential information must not be shared in unsecured or public locations.
- Any suspected security incidents must be reported immediately.

## Performance and Accountability
- Employees are accountable for meeting performance expectations, deadlines, and quality standards.
- Managers will monitor hybrid arrangements and review performance regularly.
- Changes to hybrid arrangements may be made if performance, business needs, or policies require it.

## Review and Adjustments
- Hybrid working arrangements are subject to periodic review by managers and HR.
- The company reserves the right to modify or revoke hybrid work arrangements based on business needs or policy changes.

## Policy Compliance
Failure to comply with this policy may result in corrective action in accordance with company disciplinary procedures.

## Questions
Employees should contact their manager or HR representative with any questions about this policy.
