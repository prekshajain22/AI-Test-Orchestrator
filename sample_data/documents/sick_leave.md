# Sick Leave Policy

## Purpose
To provide guidelines for requesting and approving sick leave, protecting employee health, and maintaining operational continuity.

## Scope
Applies to all employees of the organization.

## Eligibility
Employees may request sick leave when they are unable to work due to illness, injury, or a medical appointment.

## Notification
- Notify your manager as soon as possible, ideally before the start of the scheduled workday.
- Include the expected duration of absence and any relevant details.

## Approval
- Sick leave must be approved by the employee's manager.
- Approval is granted based on the employee's available sick leave balance and business needs.

## Duration and Pay
- Paid sick leave is provided according to the organization’s leave entitlement schedule.
- Unused sick leave may be carried over or managed in accordance with local law and company policy.

## Documentation
- A medical certificate or other supporting documentation may be required for absences longer than three consecutive days or when requested by management.

## Return to Work
- Employees should inform their manager of their return date as soon as it is known.
- A return-to-work discussion may be held to review any accommodations or required follow-up.

## Confidentiality
Medical information and documentation are treated as confidential and shared only with authorized personnel.

## Review
This policy is reviewed periodically and updated as needed to comply with legal requirements and business needs.
